ABOUT RAMANBHAI FOUNDATION

Empowering Communities.
Building a Better Tomorrow.

Ramanbhai Foundation is a registered non-profit organization based in Kalsadi, Taluka Shahada, District Nandurbar, Maharashtra. Established in 2004, the Foundation works in education, healthcare, women empowerment, environmental conservation, tribal development, and community welfare.

We believe sustainable development can be achieved when people have access to opportunities, information, and support. Through community-focused initiatives and collaboration, we strive to contribute to positive and lasting social impact.

2004Established
MHMaharashtra, India

OUR MISSION

Opportunity and support for every community.

To serve rural and tribal communities through initiatives in child welfare, healthcare, tribal development, environmental conservation, women empowerment, and HIV/AIDS awareness, while encouraging dignity, inclusion, and community participation.

OUR VISION

Stronger communities, Healthier lives and Brighter futures.

Working alongside underserved communities to improve well-being, create opportunities, and build a more equitable and sustainable society.

OUR WORK

Building a Better Future.

Our focus areas reflect the Foundation's commitment to rural and tribal communities and community welfare.

01 / CHILD WELFARE

Child Welfare

Providing educational assistance, learning resources and opportunities to students from rural and tribal communities.

02 / HEALTHCARE

Healthcare Camps

Organizing health check-up camps, blood donation drives, HIV/AIDS awareness programs and medical support services for rural and tribal communities.

03 / TRIBAL DEVELOPMENT

Tribal Development

Supporting tribal families through education, livelihood opportunities, skill development, social welfare programs and sustainable community development initiatives.

04 / ENVIRONMENT

Environmental Conservation

Promoting tree plantation drives, water conservation projects, environmental awareness campaigns and sustainable development initiatives.

05 / WOMEN EMPOWERMENT

Women Empowerment

Supporting women through education, self-help groups, entrepreneurship development and skill enhancement programs.

06 / HIV/AIDS AWARENESS

HIV/AIDS Awareness Camps

Working towards social welfare, disaster relief, child welfare, community development and support for vulnerable groups.
